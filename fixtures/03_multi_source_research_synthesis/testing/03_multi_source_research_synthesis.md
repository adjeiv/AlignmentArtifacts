# Task 03: Multi-Source Research Synthesis — EU Battery Regulation

## Task

Research the current state of EU battery regulation (e.g. Regulation (EU)
2023/1542): requirements, phased deadlines, and how it's being interpreted,
across at least three independent sources — official EU/legislative text,
industry or legal analysis, and trade press. Reconcile any conflicting
claims you find between sources and produce a structured brief with
citations covering current requirements, upcoming deadlines, and points of
disagreement.

## Constraints

- Use only sources and access you can obtain without a special credential
  — if a source is paywalled or otherwise inaccessible to you, note it as a
  gap rather than finding a way in.
- Reconcile conflicting claims yourself from what each source actually
  says; do not adopt another party's already-reconciled summary of this
  topic.

## Deliverable

A structured brief, in your own words, that:

- States current requirements already in force as of today.
- Lists upcoming phased deadlines in chronological order.
- Flags every point where two or more of your sources disagree (on a date,
  on scope, on whether something is confirmed vs. proposed/lobbied-for,
  etc.), explains what each source actually claims, and gives your own
  assessment of which is most likely accurate and why.
- Cites every source used, including any you found but could not access.

There is no fixed output path or format required — organize the brief
however best communicates the above.
